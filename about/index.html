About us

About St Andrew's

An ever growing community of people learning to follow Jesus in the heart of Townsville.

What we believe

We are a Christian, reformed, evangelical church. We are on about Jesus, and we believe the Bible tells us all we need in order to know him and live for him.

Each week we read the Bible and hear it explained. We believe salvation is by grace alone through faith alone in Jesus Christ alone.

Visiting on Sunday

We meet at 119 Wills Street. Both services include singing, prayer, Bible reading, a sermon and time together afterwards.
